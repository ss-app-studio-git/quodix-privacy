Quodix Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Quodix is an Android app for tradespeople to write quotes and invoices, take card payments, and keep job photos. This page describes what data the app handles, where it is kept, and why. Quodix is provided by Stanislav Shestak ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Your quotes, invoices, clients and photos are stored on your phone. The app works fully without an account.
  • If you sign in, we keep a small account record. If you are on the Pro plan, a backup of your quotes, invoices, clients and business details is kept in our cloud database.
  • Photos and signatures never go to our servers. Pro can copy photos to your own Google Drive, which only you control.
  • Card payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDK in the app. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Data stored on your phone

Everything you create in Quodix lives in the app's private storage on your device: your business profile (name, address, phone, email, logo, colors and footer text), your clients (names, phone numbers, email and postal addresses, notes), quotes and invoices with their line items and totals, payment status, job photos, and signatures captured on the phone. You can export all of it at any time from Account → Your data, with or without an account; the export is shared through Android's own share sheet, at your direction.

Android's own device backup may include the app's data in your Google account backup, as it does for most apps. That backup is run and stored by Google under your Google account, not by us.

Account (optional)

An account is needed only for card payments and cloud backup. You can sign in with Google or with an email address and password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your email address, a user ID, and (for Google sign-in) your Google account's name and profile picture link. Passwords are stored by Firebase in hashed form; we cannot read them.

Our account record holds your user ID, the ID of your Stripe account if you connect one, your plan status (for example, whether Pro is active and when it ends), and the ID of the phone currently set as your main phone for backup.

Cloud backup (Pro plan)

On the Pro plan, Quodix backs up your business details, clients, and quotes and invoices (including line items, totals, payment status and payment links) to Google Cloud Firestore, in the United States. Photos, signature images and your logo are not included. The backup exists so you can restore your records onto a new phone.

If your Pro plan ends, the backup is kept for 45 days after the end of your paid period (or 37 days after a payment failure puts the subscription on hold) and is then deleted; the app warns you before this happens. Everything stays on your phone either way.

Photo backup to your Google Drive (Pro, optional)

If you turn it on, Quodix copies your job photos and signatures into a "Quodix photos" folder in your own Google Drive. The app asks for Google's drive.file permission, which only lets it see files it created itself; it cannot see anything else in your Drive. The files go straight from your phone to Google Drive and never pass through our servers. You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google account's third-party connections; files already in your Drive stay yours.

Card payments (Stripe)

If you set up payments, you connect your own Stripe account through Stripe's onboarding. Stripe collects the identity, business and bank details it needs to pay you, under Stripe's Privacy Policy; we receive only your Stripe account ID and its status (for example, whether it can accept charges).

When your customer pays by a payment link or by tapping a card on your phone (Tap to Pay), the card details are handled entirely by Stripe. We never receive full card numbers. The app keeps the payment link and, for a tap payment, the card application name and ID that appear on an EMV receipt.

Tap to Pay requires your phone's approximate location, which Stripe uses to confirm where in-person payments happen, as card networks require. The app asks for location permission only when you start a tap payment. We do not store your location.

Subscriptions (Google Play)

Plans are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see your payment card. To confirm a Pro subscription, our server checks the purchase with Google and stores the purchase token linked to your account, along with the subscription's status and dates. The app links a purchase to your account with a one-way hash of your user ID, not your email address.

App integrity

Quodix uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity to confirm that requests to our servers come from a genuine copy of the app. This sends device and app integrity signals to Google; it does not identify you personally.

Permissions and why they're needed

  • Camera — to take job photos inside the app. Photos are saved in the app's private storage.
  • Approximate location — required by Stripe for Tap to Pay; asked only when you take a tap payment.
  • Notifications — to warn you about your plan, for example before a cloud backup is deleted or when a payment needs attention.
  • Audio settings — requested by Stripe's Tap to Pay software, which the app includes for in-person card payments.

Your clients' information

The details you enter about your own customers are yours. You decide what to record, and you are responsible for having a lawful reason to keep it. We do not use your customers' details for any purpose of our own and never contact them; the only messages they receive are the quotes, invoices and payment links you choose to send.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers that run the features above — Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Google Play, and Google Drive at your request) and Stripe — and only for those purposes, or where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

Deleting your data

  • Delete account (Account screen) deletes your sign-in, our account record and your cloud backup, and deactivates any open payment links. It does not touch the data on your phone, your Google Drive photos, or your Stripe account, which you can close from Stripe.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on the phone. Export first if you want a copy.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play.

You can also ask us to delete your account or tell you what we hold about you at the address below.

Security

Data in our cloud is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. Access rules let each account read and write only its own records, and our server alone can change plan status.

Children's privacy

Quodix is a business tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
